# Variation in shaping readiness

On ten small requests, the stored release and an unpatched repeat each ran once with Sol at low effort and no answerer. The stored result was 6/10 ready; the repeat was 7/10. 2 cases changed from not ready to ready and 1 from ready to not ready. This measures run-to-run variation, not a candidate improvement. Intermediate refusals could be repaired; readiness requires no terminal refusal or question.

The elapsed times include failed attempts. The shared credit balance showed no movement during these launches; exact attributable spend is unknown. The small Go and Node workspaces are reconstructions whose byte identity with the original study is unverified. No held-out or complex requests, Build stage, or hidden checks were run. Landing and hidden-check outcomes are unmeasured.

Both runs used [the same release source](https://github.com/KogenAI/kogen/tree/47236bbb93385bc960fd2ac353c9b000bd6ed571) and [shaping source](https://github.com/KogenAI/kogen-shaping/tree/0484edf817f6dc74f691f44d5234cf7f6ae0435e). One paired repetition is too small to characterize the full distribution of variation.
