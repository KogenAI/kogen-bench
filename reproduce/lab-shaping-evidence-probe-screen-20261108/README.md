# Screen of shaping evidence and probe handling

This candidate-only screen compared two repetitions of the same ten small requests against stored current-release cells. Candidate readiness was 15/20 (8/10 and 7/10), versus 15/20 (8/10 and 7/10) for the stored control, with 1 paired gains and 1 paired losses. The pre-registered keep rule required at least 18/20 and no paired regressions; the disposition is drop. All 20 cells per arm produced a valid Intent. Acceptance that separates correct from incorrect implementations, downstream landing, and hidden-grader performance were not measured; per-task fields are marked unmeasured. Wall times include failed attempts.

The scratch change combined conservative path-and-line citation normalization before the existing tracked-file/read checks with passing successful no-injected-file probe text to the decision checker, requiring probe quotes to appear in the observed output and in the decision statement alongside the command.
