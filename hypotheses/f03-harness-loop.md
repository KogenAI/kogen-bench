## Design implications

Keep harness choice, provider, prompt, tool surface, model effort, and whole-route policy as separate factors. For task outcomes, compare exact cell IDs and official grades; for runtime, report host, load, epoch, and paired balance. Public delivery manifests establish only that a candidate record was captured, not that the task passed.

## What this evidence does not show

These records do not establish a general Pi, Codex, kh, khr, OpenCode, Claude Code, shell-only, parallel-agent, or Grok winner. A source-reported pilot, a candidate-delivery count, an offline/scripted check, and a completed graded Build are distinct evidence types. The listed interim and confounded rows cannot support a measured superiority claim.

## Smallest open tests

Run a preregistered matched-task harness panel with exact model/effort, prompts, tools, retries, host/epoch, official grades, usage, and all attempted-cell dispositions. Then use one-factor arms for the verification gate, tool surface, prompt, steering, and helper topology, with a transfer task set held out from tuning.
