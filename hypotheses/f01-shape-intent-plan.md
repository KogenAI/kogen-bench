## Design implications

Keep Shaping and Build as separate measured stages when the question concerns shaping cost, probes, detail, or approved-Intent reliability. State the builder model and effective effort, distinguish shaped from provided Intent, and score every assigned cell using a predeclared ITT rule. A stop, test gate, provider timeout, setup fault, and official grade are separate outcomes.

## What this evidence does not show

The listed pages do not establish that more detail always helps, that a witness guarantees a successful Build, that shaping is worth its cost, or that any one plan recipe beats direct Codex or Sol. The descriptive R72 summary and the withdrawn R74 record are not current-version superiority evidence. Offline audits of Intent coverage, test strictness, and saved candidates do not estimate a live shaping effect.

## Smallest open tests

Run a preregistered, same-task comparison of provided and shaped Intents with a fixed builder, effective effort, harness, checks, and cost accounting; record the exact cell IDs and failed/invalid dispositions. Separately randomize a fixed probe budget and a no-probe condition, and test a specified approval rule prospectively before making any Build-reliability guarantee.
