# F04 — Models, efforts and routing

Coverage: H39–H50. Model and effort effects are task-, role-, and version-specific. Reported small screens remain separate from official joined outcomes. Wave B claim IDs link to [`results/claim-ledger.jsonl`](../results/claim-ledger.jsonl); a source-reported claim is not reproducible simply because it has a stable claim ID.

## Design implications

Compare model and effort at a fixed role, task set, harness, prompt, attempt budget, and host/epoch. Record requested and effective effort separately; an effort clamp changes the treatment. Include cost and wall time as separate outcomes and preserve stopped or invalid assignments in the declared analysis population.

## What this evidence does not show

The exploratory x-models and x-ladder summaries do not establish a default model, effort curve, Astra policy, or model cascade. R59’s historical anchor, R64’s effort clamp, R66’s unidentified historical control, R68b’s confounds, and withdrawn R74 cannot be combined into one frontier. Routing agreement alone does not establish better Build outcomes.

## Smallest open tests

Run a preregistered task-stratified matrix with Luna and Sol effort levels plus a capped Astra arm, recording effective settings, usage, grades, time, and cost on exact IDs. Separately compare one fixed direct route, text-based routing, and failure-triggered escalation under the same per-task budget and a held-out confirmation set.
