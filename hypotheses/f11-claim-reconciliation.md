# F11 — Claimed cross-cutting results

**Status: INTERIM.** This family collects historical summaries that span experiments; a summary is not an additional experiment. The claims below stay separate from the round records they may describe. The L0 reconciliation adds reproducible analysis from published sanitized CSVs for selected R72, R74, R69, and R57 populations. That does not imply every row is independently joined to the canonical official outcome export; each row below preserves its remaining join and scope limits.

## Hypotheses

- **H111 — Staged and direct model comparison.** On a shared task set, a staged Kogen route may help a weaker builder solve failures while direct Sol high may have a higher pass rate, lower cost per pass, or longer wall time. Falsify with exact task-matched outcomes, cost, and wall receipts for every arm.
- **H112 — Route cost and quality frontier.** Across a shared task set, direct Sol, route-v3, and direct Luna max may occupy different pass-rate and cost-per-pass points. Falsify with one reconciled cohort, compatible cost accounting, and task-level outcomes.
- **H113 — R72 same-model comparison.** On R72 P1, the Kogen Luna-max ladder may outperform direct Codex Luna max. Falsify with a complete cell join and a prespecified paired analysis.
- **H114 — R70 language choice.** The R70 implementation-stack comparison and selected RvE sample may identify a better language for Kogen. Evaluate with a parity-controlled comparison whose task, prompt, harness, and repair loop are matched; a selected sample alone cannot determine a broad language winner.
- **H115 — Cache-hit explanation.** A Kogen cache-hit rate below Codex's may explain much of the token-spend difference. Falsify with request-level records from matched epochs and compatible cache/token definitions, then relate cache changes to cost.
- **H116 — Cache-change effect.** A cache change may increase cache use. Falsify with a defined metric and baseline, matched request traffic, and before/after records.

## Design implications

- Keep each historical claim tied to its original cohort and distinguish a Kogen recipe from a direct-model baseline.
- Keep R70 original, rerun, extension, task-eight smoke, and compile-time measurements as separate endpoints and cohorts.
- Keep cache telemetry separate from Build efficacy, and require matched epochs and compatible token accounting before explaining cost differences.

## Explicit non-implications

- The source-reported R72 difference does not establish superiority or a current-version effect.
- The R70 cohorts do not establish a broad language ranking or select a Kogen implementation language.
- T98's cache smoke does not show that a cache change increases success or lowers cost; T100 provides no evidence because it did not run.
- None of the historical cross-cutting percentages adds new experimental observations beyond its underlying run or claim record.

## Smallest open tests

- Reconcile the historical H111/H112 claims to immutable cell IDs, official outcomes, task pairs, full pins, model/effort receipts, and compatible cost/wall inputs; otherwise retire the numeric comparisons.
- For a language decision, register a matched implementation cohort with identical task behavior, prompt, agent loop, tools, and repair budget; include exact harness pins and a complete ITT join.
- For cache claims, run a fixed request workload before and after the change and publish sanitized request-level token/cache records with the metric definition and denominator.
