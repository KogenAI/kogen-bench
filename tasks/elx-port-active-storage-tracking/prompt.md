We need a way to track storage usage for each organization and board in Trackline. We only need to count ticket images and files embedded in board descriptions, ticket descriptions, and comments. A board's usage includes everything counted on that board and its tickets. An organization's usage includes everything counted across all of its boards.

Add up-to-date `Trackline.Ports.Storage.bytes_used/1` values for organizations and storage boards, calculated in the background. This must work for small organizations and for those with lots of attachments and many active users. Uploading, replacing or deleting an attachment must eventually update the values; reading usage must not scan the attachments in a request.

The base models blobs separately from attachment links. Count `Board/description`, `Ticket/image`, `Ticket/description`, and `Comment/body` links. A ticket belongs to `storage_board_id`; a comment belongs to its ticket. Other kinds of attachments (avatars, exports, etc.) do not count. The same blob linked more than once on a board counts once there; organization usage is the sum of its board usages. Keep `attach(attrs, organization_id)`, `detach(attachment, organization_id)`, `enqueue/1`, `bytes_used/1`, and the Oban worker `Trackline.Ports.RecountStorage` on the default queue. Background jobs may be retried or arrive after an attachment has changed. Existing application tests must keep passing.
