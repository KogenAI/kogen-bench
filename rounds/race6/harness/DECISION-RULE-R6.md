# DECISION-RULE-R6: Race 6, spec-format lever pilot (Elixir and Go)

Status: PILOT. Two Mac runs per condition, one per Mac, in one time-boxed wave. Direction labels describe separation of the observed Mac FINAL scores under the rule below. They do not establish statistical significance, a population-level direction, or general superiority.

## Question
How do the four supplied specification-and-tool bundles compare on official hidden-suite FINAL scores for Elixir and, separately, for Go in this pilot? Conditions are compared WITHIN each language only; race 6 makes no cross-language ranking claim. B and D additionally receive the pinned Quint CLI, and D includes remaining race 5 specification files. The comparisons do not isolate document format, equalize information content, or identify a Quint-by-Gherkin interaction.

## Measure and report
- **PRIMARY:** use official FINAL scored-case counts, integers from 0 through 106. For each condition, an official median requires every planned deciding slot to be valid and scored. With two counts s1 <= s2, median = (s1+s2)/2. Preserve half-case values without rounding, and report the median as cases out of 106. If any planned slot is invalid or missing, report the condition's median as unavailable and show the available per-run scores without computing a substitute median.
- **Differences:** B−A, C−A and D−A mean differences between the official condition medians, in cases, not percentages. A difference is unavailable if either median is unavailable. Also report each available within-host, within-wave X−A difference, identified by host and wave; these paired differences are descriptive and do not replace the direction criterion.
- **Spread:** maximum minus minimum official FINAL count across the condition's complete planned deciding sample. An incomplete sample's official spread is unavailable. The "ambiguous pilot" label uses only complete samples.
- **Direction wording:** for each X in B, C and D, if X or A lacks a complete planned deciding sample, report “insufficient data”. Otherwise report “X above A” exactly when min(X) > max(A), and “X below A” exactly when max(X) < min(A), using all planned deciding runs. In every other complete-data case report “no clear direction”. Any equality across the two score ranges prevents strict separation; equal scores within one condition do not themselves prevent separation. Equal medians give a zero median difference and no clear direction. These labels describe the observed pilot only.
- **Report:** every run's validity status and reason, official FINAL count, condition median and spread, median differences, cumulative tokens at FINAL, wall time and build/test time. State timing and token measurement methods, missing observations, and raw versus stall-adjusted time. These resource measures do not break ties or determine direction.
- **Reported only:** T+30 snapshot scores and Linux results. Never use these to select a FINAL or supply a missing deciding score.
- Do not name an overall winner. The top-two median gap is used only for the "ambiguous pilot" label; race 5's time-to-target priority and build/test-time/spread tie breakers do not apply.

## Scoring
- Score offline with the frozen scorer only after the wave is DONE, all its arm processes and descendants have stopped, and FINAL snapshots are complete. Never run scoring or scorer warm-up on a host with a live arm, including an arm from another wave. Suite access for scoring must not expose it to any live arm on any host. Asynchronous scheduling does not override these requirements; delay or omit a later wave under its frozen deadline and disclose the outcome.
- Retain executable FINAL and T+30 snapshots until scoring finishes. The T+30 score uses the snapshot 30 minutes after the host's epoch. If a checkpoint is missing, incomplete or source-only, report its score as unavailable; do not interpolate, substitute a later snapshot, rebuild it into a purported historical executable, or assign zero for missing artifacts.
- Score every retained designated snapshot over the complete frozen inventory; report both total-inventory and scored-case denominators. Use at most three concurrent arm-snapshots per host, the shared host slot pool, and `--jobs 2` per scorer. Preserve race 5's 60-second invocation limits, process-group/cwd cleanup, and one untimed version warm-up of at most 900 seconds per raw scoring. Keep its offline proxy/environment controls and self-contained prebuilt-deliverable requirements.
- For every designated snapshot, including FINAL and T+30, if the FIRST scored-case count is 103 through 106 inclusive, run a SECOND scoring. A case officially passes only if it passes in both raw scorings; intersect case IDs, not aggregate counts. Otherwise the first scoring is official. Preserve raw, combined and flip receipts and disclose flaky cases. Apply the same procedure to reported Linux snapshots. No discretionary extra scoring or best-of retry is allowed.
- An implementation failure that the frozen scorer validly records as case failures retains that score. A FINAL that cannot be scored because of a harness failure is invalid with no official numeric score. Preserve and disclose the failure evidence; do not manufacture a zero or silently omit the run.

## Invalidation
- **Scope:** an arm-run means one condition on one host in one wave. An invalid arm-run has no official score and is reported as invalid, not as “not reached”. Invalid and missing runs follow the completeness rule; neither counts as zero. Keep the underlying observations and audit evidence, clearly marked non-deciding. If the whole round is invalidated, report “invalid round”; do not issue direction labels or a score-triggered continuation decision from it.
- **Invalidates one arm-run:** wrong or missing model/effort receipts; an agent-issued call to a real model provider; a protected outside read or attempted read; a Kogen denylist access event; hidden-suite access as defined below; modification of that arm's frozen spec/, INTERFACE.md or TARGET.md; or a FINAL unscorable for a harness reason. The agent's authorized Codex model transport is not an agent-issued provider call. Merely writing a provider URL into source is not a call.
- **Hidden-suite access:** during a live wave, an arm must not request, retrieve, read, execute, import, or search for actual suite artifacts, cases, expected outputs, traces or runner code, including earlier published versions, copies, archives, caches and mirrors. Blocked and unsuccessful attempts count. This applies through tools, child processes, symlinks, relative paths and aliases as well as direct file or network access. Frozen condition-approved documents and the common interface remain permitted inputs even when they contain suite-related identifiers; that permission does not extend to following their references into protected artifacts.
- **Audit classification:** freeze the protected-artifact inventory, prohibited repository/domain patterns and exact suite case-ID prefix patterns before launch. Match local access against protected artifacts and resolved paths; do not define protected status from a generic basename alone. `conformance`, `conformance/`, `cases/quint`, a known filename, or a suite-related string appearing solely in an agent-created test, a source write, a command comment or an allowed document is not by itself a violation. Agent-created tests with those names are permitted unless they access or incorporate prohibited artifacts. Command matching must distinguish executed access/search operations and their targets from inert text. A raw substring match alone is a flag to classify, not sufficient evidence to void.
- **Network audit:** retain race 5's denylist for KogenAI repositories, `github.com/.../kogen*`, kogen.dev, www.kogen.dev and bench.kogen.dev, and extend it to suite repositories identified by kogen-bench or kogen-conformance. Agent-issued network access/search targets, returned web-result URLs and opened-page URLs matching the frozen prohibited-resource patterns or case-ID prefixes are voiding, including blocked attempts. Network search queries containing the frozen case-ID prefixes are voiding. Searching permitted local documents or agent-created tests is allowed. Merely seeing those strings in permitted local documents or unrelated returned text is not an access event. Known copies and mirrors remain prohibited even when their names do not match these strings. Record the event, resolved target and matched rule for each invalidation.
- **Scorer exemption:** only the supervisor's separately identified post-stop scoring and integrity-check processes may access the hidden suite. Race 5's permission for an arm to run local `conformance/bin/kogen-conformance` is revoked. No scoring output or unlocked artifact may become available to a live arm.
- **Invalidates the whole round:** modification after freeze of the supervisor's frozen rule, suite/scorer or inventory, split, MODE, briefs, common interface/target, kits or harness; different frozen versions between hosts or waves; a lock/isolation failure making suite artifacts or another arm's implementation available to a live arm; or wrong model/effort receipts in more than one arm-run. Missing receipts alone invalidate the affected runs. Arm-local document tampering follows the one-arm-run rule unless it also changes shared frozen inputs or exposes another arm.
- **Host drops:** a dropped host's slots are reported missing; a host drop alone does not invalidate the round.
