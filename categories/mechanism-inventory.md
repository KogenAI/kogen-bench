# Kogen mechanism inventory

This page lists the 80 parts of Kogen and of its build loop that someone could change, and says for each one what the benchmark evidence on it is. It is a map of what has been measured and what has not. It is not a ranking of what to change, and nothing here is a recommendation.

**Scope.** The list was made by reading Kogen at commit [`e2e8196e18facc62b258b29adb6e16ab149f0cbb`](https://github.com/KogenAI/kogen/commit/e2e8196e18facc62b258b29adb6e16ab149f0cbb) (28 October 2026), including the build loop it bundles (`cli/third_party/kogen-loop`, pinned by `cli/loop-pin.json`). One row is one part that can be changed on its own: a setting in `project.yaml`, a constant in the code, a prompt or a policy. Paths and line numbers in the table are in that commit. The `project.yaml` settings and `kogen` commands named in the table are the ones the commit documents; environment variables that only carry setup are not parts. The list was made by reading source; no model was run for it.

## How to read the table

- **Status** says how the part's evidence relates to the current code. *Measured on current code*: a round or run varied the part, and the code that defines it has not changed since the last measured Kogen commit (test files ignored). *Measured on older code*: it was varied, but the code changed since, or the only measurement was on Kogen Lab (a separate re-implementation of Kogen's loop, used for screening, whose equivalence to Kogen is not measured), on the older Elixir-based Kogen (before the Go core replaced it on 24 October), on direct model calls, or on a research patch that is not in Kogen. *Partly measured*: only an interim result or a model-free profile exists. *Never measured*: no round varied or isolated the part.
- **A status is not a verdict.** "Measured on current code" says the code is unchanged, not that the finding is decision-grade. Most evidence here is observation: one round, a few repetitions, single models and research-made tasks. Each linked round page states its own limits.
- **Evidence** links the public round page where there is one. "Internal measurement, not yet published" marks findings from internal runs and analyses whose records are not in this repository. Their numbers are reported as given and cannot be checked here.
- Model names are written as in the round pages (Sol, Terra, Luna: OpenAI models; Sonnet, Opus, Haiku: Claude models). Changes labelled "research patch" were built for a test and are not in Kogen.
- **What it could change** names what a change to the part could affect: success (hidden-test pass rate), cost, speed or caching.

**Correction, 29 October 2026.** Review times have been recalculated as elapsed review phases, including tool work. The evidence-status counts are unchanged. Paired results use the same task and repetition in each arm, evaluator infrastructure failures remain ungraded, and missing usage remains missing. Some historical replay totals and blind grades remain unverified; follow the linked profile's correction before citing them.

Six of the parts do not exist in Kogen at all and are research patches only: the default read window and outline read, the edit tool, the unchanged-read and write stubs, the stale tool-output elision, the reset ledger and the extra Anthropic cache breakpoint. They are listed because rounds measured them.

The status counts were first made as of the commit above and are updated with the results of 29 October. Thirteen parts are "partly measured" because results arrived after the first count. Four came first: the shaper's request audit and decision check (interim results in [the flawed-requests round](../rounds/shape-flawed-requests-1/README.md)), and the checkpoint frequency and provider retry policy ([two model-free profiles](mechanism-profiles.md)). Six more moved from "never measured" after [further model-free analyses of run logs](mechanism-profiles.md): the invalid-submission limit, the builder tool set, the no-progress stop, the round limit, the no-progress fingerprint hook and the per-call response budget. Three more moved on 29 October, described below. Two parts that were already measured gained evidence without a change of status: the tool-result cut and the review verdict handling, and the review source delivery row records an observation from a comparison that is still running.

The results of 29 October, in [Climb results, 29 October](mechanism-profiles.md#climb-results-29-october), changed five statuses. Two parts moved to "measured on current code" because a comparison on the pinned Kogen commit varied them in the shipped code: the builder model and effort and the reviewer model and effort. Three parts moved to "partly measured": the verification feedback format (from "measured on older code") and the cache identity scope (from "never measured"), because they were measured only through research patches that are not in Kogen, and the step self-check (from "never measured"), after a model-free replay. Other rows gained evidence without a change of status: the shaper reasoning effort (an interim look), the no-progress stop, the tool-result cut and the per-call response budget. These are results under rules written before the cells or interim looks, not verdicts, and the status line above still applies.

## Additional findings, 31 October 2026

### Native macOS sandbox options, measured 15 October (historical)

In short: with the network open, nono 0.79.0 and codex 0.162.1 passed Kogen's first-version containment natively on macOS, sandbox-runtime 0.0.78 failed, the first pinned Kogen compiler refused the policy, and a newer Kogen release passed. No tool stopped leftover processes or had a wall deadline.

This was measured on the sandbox of the retired earlier Kogen, on one Mac laptop, with three repetitions per cell and no model. Outside writes, writes to the shared temp folder and a planted secret read were blocked in 3 of 3 repetitions for every sandbox in its configured form. The result describes those tool versions on that day, not today's Kogen. Details, configurations, limits and the harness are in [the pack](../reproduce/containment-macos-native-20261015/README.md).

## Limits

- Evidence is observation unless a round page says a rule written beforehand was met. One-round results (the review-value, builder-helper and Kogen Lab screens) are single-model, research-made tasks with one or a few attempts per setting.
- The status compares code, not behaviour. A part measured on older code may still behave the same, and a part measured on current code may not generalise to other tasks.
- Where a round page says only "a development build" or "uncommitted source", the exact commit that ran is not known, and the status rests on the named base commit.
- Runs in progress on 28 October (a shaper, reviewer and builder comparison on the ChatGPT route, the request audit and decision check comparison, and further repetitions of a Terra against Sonnet builder comparison) have no final result and are cited as queued or interim, except where the results of 29 October say otherwise. On 29 October the second look of the shaper effort comparison and the follow-up on reviews larger than the context window were still running.
- Records marked "Internal measurement, not yet published" are summarised from internal notes. They are reported, not recomputable from this repository.
