# Privacy

This notice covers the public research website at bench.kogen.dev.

## Hosting and analytics

Cloudflare Pages hosts this website. Cloudflare receives the network information needed to deliver pages and protect the site. See the [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for details.

The production site uses Plausible for aggregate pageview analytics. Local development and private previews do not load analytics. The [Plausible data policy](https://plausible.io/data-policy) explains how it collects and handles analytics data.

## Preferences, email, and external links

The color-theme preference is stored in your browser so the site can remember your chosen appearance. It is not sent as an analytics event.

If you email [contact@kogen.dev](mailto:contact@kogen.dev), your message and the contact details you provide are handled through the email service used for that conversation. External websites have their own privacy notices. For questions about privacy on this site, [contact Kogen](/contact/).
